## Reading: the fastest card is not the fastest machine

At 768×432 the RTX 5090 takes **0.111 s per frame against 0.083 s on the
RTX 4070 and 0.071 s on the RTX 5070**, and its tensor ratio is 1.08 where
the 3060's is 1.49. The record says why: with **128 torch threads** on a
61-core quota, the 1-minute load during the jobs is 25–50 (the host's own
load was 3.6 before), the CPU-side part of the frame (Multi-HMR's
preprocessing, the tracker, the conversion) runs over-subscribed, and the
GPU is idle most of the frame (≤ 27 % utilisation in the readings). The
frame is bounded by the host's CPU regime, not by the card, and torch's
default thread count on a 256-thread box is a bad regime. Protocol v2 §10
fixes that the thread count is *recorded and not tuned*, so this is the
number, reported with its cause beside it (ADR-0006 §6: a property of the
backend and host). A future amendment could pin `torch.set_num_threads` to
the quota or to the physical cores of the *instance*; it would have to
rerun every machine from a new commit, and is the author's call.
