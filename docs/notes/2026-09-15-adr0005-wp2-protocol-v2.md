# 2026-09-15 - WP2, a performance protocol that can be the final one

WP2 of ADR-0005. Protocol v2 for RQ1/RQ2 is frozen in
`docs/experiments/protocol-v2.md` and implemented; the v1 matrix is the pilot
and stays as written. This note is the design record; no measurement has been
taken under v2 yet.

## What v1 could not tell apart, and what v2 does about it

ADR-0005 §2.3 is the list. One decision each.

**A suite, and a process per job.** `experiments/benchmark/v2/*.toml` fixes
the policy once - warm-up, measured frames, repetitions, frame selection, the
seed - and lists the cells. Every (cell, repetition) is a job, and
`unproject benchmark-suite` spawns `unproject benchmark-job` for each in a
fresh interpreter, through the same `sys.executable` the driver runs under.
So every repetition loads the model itself, every memory figure belongs to
one job, and nothing - weights, Metal allocator, a warmed cache - survives
from one to the next. The driver never imports torch.

**A shuffled, recorded order.** `plan_jobs` interleaves the repetitions of
all cells and shuffles with `order_seed`; each result carries its index in
that order and the seed, and the driver writes `suite-run.json` after every
job with the exit code and the file it produced. A slow drift of the machine
lands on every cell rather than the last one, and an interrupted run still
says what ran.

**The same sixty frames everywhere, spread along the clip.** `select_frames`
takes the first five frames as warm-up and spreads sixty over frames 5–374,
monotone for the tracker. The list is in every result and the summary marks
a cell whose jobs disagree.

**Phases apart, on frames already in memory.** The job decodes its frames
from the ingest artifact before any clock starts, then times model load and
session open once each, and per frame: inference on the array, the rest
bodies a mesh cell sends once per new track, and the wire message. The
message is built by `unproject.bodies.wire.frame_message`, which moved from
the API package to the pipeline so that the socket and the benchmark
serialise through one function; `unproject_api.live.to_wire` is now that
function. The number the benchmark reports for serialisation is the number
the socket pays, and its byte size is recorded.

**Memory with a baseline.** RSS before the model is built, after, at the
end, and the high-water mark - so the model's footprint is a difference, not
a whole-process number that includes the interpreter and the frames held for
timing. Metal counters at baseline and at their maximum over the frames.

**Throughput as measured.** Steady frames over the wall clock of the steady
phase, per job. A run that stalled once shows it; a reciprocal of the median
would not.

**Letterbox, not crop.** The square cells pad 1920×1080 to 1920×1920. Every
person is in both cells, and because Multi-HMR resizes the long edge to 768
whatever the source, a person covers the same tensor pixels in both; the
tensor differs by the 336 padded rows and nothing else. A discovery worth
writing down: the *resolution* factor does not change the tensor at all -
960×540 reaches the model as the same 768×432 - which is what v1's
"resolution barely matters" meant and could not say. Every job records the
tensor it actually fed the model (`model_input_size`).

**Provenance on the footage.** `ExperimentConfig` gains an optional
`[provenance]` section - title, author, URL, licence, acquisition date, the
original file's digest and size, the derivation command - excluded from
every stage identity, and `experiments/pedestrians.toml` plus its three
transformations carry it. Every job result embeds it, so a table can name
its source without a lookup.

**Conditions.** Per job, before and after: power source, low-power mode and
the thermal CPU speed limit as `pmset` reports them on macOS, raw text kept
beside the parsed values. `unknown` is a value.

**Paired differences.** The suite declares pairs of cells; the summary takes
`b − a` per frame on the same repetition and position, reports median, IQR,
the fraction of frames where b was slower and the ratio of medians, and
refuses to call the comparison paired unless both cells timed the same
source frames.

**Protocol minimums reported, not enforced.** The schema accepts a small
suite - the fixture smoke suite runs two cells at eight frames - and the
summary marks any cell below sixty frames or three complete jobs
`meets_protocol: false`. A short run is a short run.

## What was verified

- `uv run unproject benchmark-suite experiments/benchmark/v2/fixture-suite.toml --dry-run`
  runs four jobs in four processes, in the seed's order, and
  `benchmark-suite-summary` excludes all four as dry runs and writes the
  three summary files.
- 48 new tests in `test_benchmark_suite.py`: config rules, the planned order
  (deterministic, seed-driven, not the file's), frame selection (spread,
  monotone, refuses a short clip), the job (plan respected, phases apart,
  mesh pays for a rest body once per track and sends a bigger message, the
  recorded geometry, a dying estimator leaves a failed prefix), the driver
  end to end with real subprocesses (a job that cannot start is recorded and
  the suite continues; `--cell` keeps planned indices), the result contract
  (frames must follow the plan), the summary (deterministic, warm-up out,
  nearest-rank spread, throughput from wall clock, dry runs and failures
  out and visible, protocol minimums, inconsistent frames flagged, paired
  differences on the same frames), the CSVs and `pmset` parsing.
- `model_input_size` pinned for 1920×1080, 960×540, 1920×1920 and the
  fixture; `[provenance]` parsed, absent-not-invented, refuses a bad digest,
  and enters no stage identity.
- Full gate from the clean environment: 691 + 18 tests, ruff, format, mypy,
  30+ configs, lock.

## What is not done here

No measurement under v2 exists yet. The final run is the author's, on the
M3 Pro the protocol names, from a clean commit: fetch the clip per
`docs/reproduce.md` §2, make the three transformations per §4, then the two
commands. About twenty-four model loads plus 24 × 65 frames; well under an
hour. A pilot of the tooling with real inference on another machine is
allowed and is not a thesis number.
