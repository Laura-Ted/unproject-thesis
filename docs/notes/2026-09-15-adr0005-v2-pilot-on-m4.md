# 2026-09-15 - Protocol v2 run once on an M4, as a pilot of the tooling

The v2 suite (`experiments/benchmark/v2/pedestrians-suite.toml`, WP2 of
ADR-0005) ran end to end on this machine, an Apple M4 with 16 GB, from
06:04:44 to 06:36:43 UTC: 24 processes, one per (cell, repetition), in the
order seed 20260915 shuffled them, every exit code 0, every cell
`meets_protocol: true` with 180 steady frames over its three repetitions.
The results are under `artifacts/benchmark-v2/rq1rq2-v2-pedestrians/`,
gitignored, and are **not thesis numbers**, for four reasons the artifacts
record themselves:

- the machine is not the protocol's (`machine: Apple M4`; protocol v2 §2
  names the M3 Pro);
- every job records `git_dirty: true` at commit `ba11dcc` - WP3 was being
  written while it ran;
- jobs 0-5 (06:04-06:16 UTC) overlapped another session's inference campaign
  on the same GPU, and the test suites of WP3 ran alongside all of them;
- `cpu_speed_limit` is `null` before and after every job: `pmset -g therm`
  on this machine reports no CPU power status at all, so the thermal
  condition the protocol asks for is unrecorded rather than clean.

## What the run established about the tooling

- The letterboxed clips reach the model as a square: `model_input_size`
  768×768 for `pedestrians-letterbox*`, 768×432 for the 16:9 cells, at both
  source resolutions. The transformation of protocol v2 §3.2 does what it
  says, and the tensor the model saw is in every job file.
- The frames are the same across the cells of a pair (`same_frames: true`
  for all six pairs), so the paired differences are per frame on the same
  content.
- Model load is recorded apart from the frames and varies by a factor of two
  between processes (7.1 s to 14.6 s); it never enters a frame figure.
- The summary, the CSVs and the pair table regenerate from the job files with
  no inference; `unproject evidence-bundle` collected the suite into a trial
  bundle under `/tmp` and `docs/thesis-figures/generate.py --bundle` drew the
  RQ1/RQ2 figure from it. The trial was not committed and the committed
  figures, which are the author's v1 pilot, were restored.

## What it showed, to be read as a pilot and nothing more

Median inference per frame, skeleton output: 0.647 s at 1920×1080, 0.636 s
at 960×540, 1.098 s at 1920×1920, 1.072 s at 960×960. The two aspect-ratio
pairs have every one of their 180 frames slower letterboxed
(`fraction_b_slower: 1.0`, median ratio 1.70 and 1.68); the two resolution
pairs are flat (ratio 1.02 and 1.02, the full-resolution side slower on 59 %
and 64 % of frames). The direction agrees with the v1 pilot in the README - shape,
not resolution, decides the cost - and the magnitudes are not to be quoted:
the letterbox-mesh cell's p75 of 2.0 s comes from the two repetitions that
ran during the overlap (jobs 2 and 3), which is the contamination the
protocol's fresh-process rule can record but not undo.

The final run is the author's, on the M3 Pro, from a clean commit, with
nothing else on the GPU: `docs/release.md` §2.
